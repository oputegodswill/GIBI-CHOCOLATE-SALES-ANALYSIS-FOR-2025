                                 IN-ANALYSIS OBSERVATION
The sales trend analysis indicates that June recorded the highest sales at 74,794.7, while April experienced the lowest sales at 36,590.68, highlighting a significant fluctuation in monthly performance.
From Country perspective, France emerged as the top revenue-generating country with 89,956.88, whereas the United States recorded the lowest revenue at 54,082.88, suggesting regional differences in sales performance.
In terms of brand performance, Toblerone generated the highest revenue of 118,291.29, while Hershey contributed the least with 91,088.73, indicating varying brand competitiveness.
Analysis by product type shows that Chocolate Box contributed the highest revenue share at 21.12%, whereas White Chocolate accounted for the lowest share at 14.78%, reflecting differences in product preference.
Examining transaction amounts reveals that items priced between 1000–2000 accounted for the highest transaction value at 228,020.14, while items within the 4000–5000 range had the lowest transaction value at 42,495.39, suggesting higher demand for moderately priced products.
Lastly, in terms of units sold, Chocolate Box recorded the highest sales volume with 10,291 units, whereas White Chocolate had the lowest at 7,763 units, reinforcing its lower market demand.
